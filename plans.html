FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my membership anytime?

Yes! All our memberships are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can cancel anytime with 30 days notice.

Is there a joining fee?

We occasionally run promotions with waived joining fees. Contact us or visit our gym to learn about current offers.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes, you can freeze your membership for up to 3 months per year for medical reasons or extended travel.

Do you offer family discounts?

Absolutely! We offer 15% off for family memberships of 2 or more. Ask about our family plans at the front desk.
