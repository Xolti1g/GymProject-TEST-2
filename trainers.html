Meet Our Professional Trainers

Our certified trainers bring years of experience and passion to help you achieve your fitness goals.

Mike Johnson

Strength & Conditioning Coach

With over 10 years of experience in strength training, Mike specializes in helping clients build muscle and increase their overall strength. He holds certifications in NSCA-CSCS and has worked with professional athletes.

Weight Training Bodybuilding Powerlifting

Sarah Williams

Yoga & Wellness Instructor

Sarah is a certified yoga instructor with expertise in Vinyasa, Hatha, and restorative yoga. She believes in the mind-body connection and helps clients find balance through mindful movement and breathing techniques.

Yoga Meditation Flexibility

David Chen

Cardio & HIIT Specialist

David is passionate about high-intensity training and cardiovascular fitness. His energetic classes are designed to push your limits while keeping workouts fun and engaging. He specializes in fat loss and endurance building.

HIIT Cardio Circuit Training

Emma Davis

Nutrition & Fitness Expert

Emma combines her knowledge of nutrition and fitness to provide holistic coaching. She holds a degree in Sports Nutrition and helps clients develop sustainable eating habits alongside their workout routines.

Nutrition Weight Loss Meal Planning

James Martinez

CrossFit & Functional Training

James is a Level 2 CrossFit certified trainer who focuses on functional fitness. His training philosophy centers on building real-world strength and mobility that translates to everyday life activities.

CrossFit Functional Fitness Olympic Lifting

Lisa Thompson

Pilates & Core Training

Lisa is a certified Pilates instructor specializing in core strengthening and posture correction. She works with clients of all ages to improve their core stability, flexibility, and overall body awareness.

Pilates Core Training Posture
